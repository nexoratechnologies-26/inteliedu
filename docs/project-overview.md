# Inteliedu - Project Overview

## 1. Executive Summary
**Inteliedu** is an AI-powered interactive 3D learning platform that combines interactive 3D visualization, AI tutoring, digital learning materials, assessments, live classroom capabilities, voice interaction, and student analytics.

The platform transforms traditional, static digital education into an immersive, highly engaging, multimodal learning ecosystem. By unifying cutting-edge WebGL 3D model manipulation with conversational AI tutors and real-time collaboration, Inteliedu delivers personalized educational pathways for students, powerful pedagogical tools for teachers, and institutional governance for administrators.

1. **Interactive 3D Visualization**:
   - High-fidelity 3D models for complex subjects (Solar System, Human Anatomy, Mechanical & Civil Engineering).
   - Real-time manipulation (rotation, zoom, explode views, x-ray layers, interactive part labels).

2. **AI Tutor & RAG Engine**:
   - Context-aware virtual tutor backed by Gemini LLM and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG).
   - Real-time answers grounded strictly in uploaded curriculum notes, textbooks, and lesson materials.
   - Multimodal voice interaction for hands-free study sessions.

3. **Digital Learning Materials**:
   - Structured course syllabus, multimedia documents, interactive slides, and searchable knowledge bases.

4. **Continuous Assessment Engine**:
   - Automated AI quiz generation from lesson notes.
   - Assignment distribution, submission, and automated grading with actionable feedback.

5. **Live Classroom & Attendance**:
   - Real-time collaborative audio/video sessions with synchronized 3D scene sharing.
   - Automated attendance logging and student engagement tracking.

6. **Actionable Student Analytics**:
   - Granular learning telemetry, quiz performance metrics, topic mastery tracking, and teacher intervention triggers.

---

## 3. Target User Personas
- **Students**: Learners who benefit from visual 3D comprehension, conversational AI assistance, self-paced quizzes, and interactive assignments.
- **Teachers**: Educators who manage classrooms, curate 3D course materials, conduct live interactive sessions, monitor attendance, and generate AI-assisted assessments.
- **Administrators**: Institutional leads overseeing user management, department permissions, platform compliance, and aggregated performance reporting.

---

## 4. Key Value Propositions
- **Enhanced Retention**: 3D spatial interaction elevates conceptual clarity for STEM and abstract subjects.
- **24/7 Personalized Tutoring**: Instant AI guidance tailored to individual learning speeds.
- **Unified Workflow**: Eliminates fragmented toolsets by combining LMS, 3D visualization, AI assistants, and video conferencing in one modern web app.
